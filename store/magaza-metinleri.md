## English

**Title (≤30):** `QuickRemote: Slide Remote` (25)

**Short description (≤80):** `Turn your phone into a presentation remote: slides, laser, pen, notes. No ads.` (78)

**Full description:**

```
Make your phone and watch the remote for the presentation on your computer. Change slides, point with a laser, draw, and keep an eye on your notes and your time from your pocket. No ads, no account, no data collected.

TWO WAYS, BOTH WIRELESS
• Wi-Fi: install the free QuickRemote PC app on the computer (Windows and Linux), scan the QR code, and you are connected. The link stays on your local network and is encrypted with TLS.
• Bluetooth: install nothing on the computer; the phone becomes a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. There are ready-made settings for PowerPoint, LibreOffice Impress and WPS Presentation.

PRESENTATION CONTROL
• Next, previous, start, end, jump to a slide
• Jump from a grid of slide thumbnails
• Slide picture, next slide and speaker notes on your phone (presenter view, portrait and landscape)
• Black and white screen
• PDF presentations: go full screen and turn pages
• Talk timer: you choose the warning times and vibration patterns
• Time-per-slide report and history of your last 20 talks

LASER AND DRAWING
• The phone screen is a touchpad: laser pointer
• Pen, highlighter, eraser, clear all, colour picker
• Spotlight: dims the screen and lights the spot under your finger
• Clear drawings on slide change, or keep them

VOLUME AND MEDIA
• PC volume: up, down, set a level, mute
• Play, pause and skip the media that is playing
• Play and stop the video on a slide

WEAR OS WATCH
• Next/previous slide, laser and black/white screen from your wrist
• Turn slides with the rotating bezel or crown
• The talk clock on the watch, which vibrates when time is up
• The watch does not connect to the computer: it goes through QuickRemote on your phone

EXTRAS
• English and Turkish; on Android 13 and later the language is also in system settings
• Light, dark and six colour themes
• App shortcuts and a Quick Settings tile: connect to your last computer with one tap
• The remote keeps working while the phone is locked

PRIVACY
No data is sent to the developer. No analytics, no ads, no account. The camera only reads the pairing QR code.

REQUIREMENTS
For Wi-Fi mode, the QuickRemote PC app on the computer (free, github.com/berat-kaan-akcan/QuickRemote). It works with PowerPoint or WPS Presentation on Windows, and LibreOffice Impress or WPS Presentation on Linux. For Bluetooth mode the computer only needs Bluetooth.

QuickRemote is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, The Document Foundation or Kingsoft. PowerPoint, Impress and WPS are the names of their respective owners.
```
