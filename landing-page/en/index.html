Connect in one of three ways.

The PC app unlocks every feature. If you would rather not install anything, the phone or the watch connects to the computer like a keyboard.

Phone and QuickRemote PC

The way with the most features. Phone and computer need the same Wi-Fi network, not the internet.

  1. Open QuickRemote PC on the computer.
  2. Scan its QR code with the phone. Computers on the same network also show up in a list.
  3. Open your presentation and tap Start on the phone.
  • Slide number and notes
  • Laser, pen, highlighter
  • Touchpad
  • Video and volume
  • Presentation report
  • Several phones at once

Just the phone

Nothing to install on the computer. The phone connects to it as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse.

  1. Tap Connect with Bluetooth in the app.
  2. Add the phone in the computer’s Bluetooth settings. You do this once.
  3. Pick your program: PowerPoint, Impress or WPS.
  • Next, back, start, end
  • Black and white screen
  • Pen and highlighter
  • Touchpad
  • Media keys and volume

A computer never answers a keyboard, so the slide number and notes don’t reach the phone this way.

Just the watch

Your phone can stay in your pocket. The watch connects straight to the computer as a Bluetooth keyboard.

  1. Open QuickRemote on the watch and tap Add computer.
  2. Pick the watch in the computer’s Bluetooth settings and confirm the code on both.
  3. Turn the bezel or tap the screen.
  • Next and back
  • Start and end
  • Black and white screen
  • A buzz for every key

Your phone does more than a clicker.

These come with QuickRemote PC, over Wi-Fi.

Everything stays on your network.

A new PIN every time

The QR code vouches for the PC
The phone expects the certificate in the QR code. Without one, it asks you to compare the security code.
A limit on guesses
After 5 wrong PINs, that device has to wait 60 seconds.

Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Ask on GitHub.

Does it cost anything?

No, QuickRemote is free, and you don’t need an account.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. In the Wi-Fi mode the phone and the computer only need to share a network. The Bluetooth and watch modes don’t need a network at all.

Does it work with an iPhone or Apple Watch?

Not yet. The remote runs on Android and the watch app on Wear OS.

Which watches does it work with?

Watches running Wear OS 3 or later. The watch has to be able to act as a Bluetooth keyboard; the app checks this when it opens and tells you if it can’t. The bezel changes slides on a Galaxy Watch, the crown on a Pixel Watch.

Do I have to install anything on the computer?

The Wi-Fi mode needs QuickRemote PC. For Bluetooth and the watch you install nothing: the computer sees the phone or the watch as a keyboard.

Can I present a PDF?

Yes. In the Wi-Fi mode it opens full screen in Acrobat, SumatraPDF, Okular and browsers, and turns the pages. On Linux it can also turn a PDF open in LibreOffice into a presentation.

Can several phones connect at once?

Yes. The phone that picks up the laser or the pen first keeps it until its stroke ends. If two people press Next at the same moment, the slide moves on only once.

Does the connection drop when the phone locks?

No. With the remote open, the connection keeps going in the background while the phone is locked.

Which languages does it support?

English and Turkish. The apps follow your device’s language, and you can change it in the settings.
