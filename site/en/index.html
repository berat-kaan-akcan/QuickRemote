Move the pointer over the screen

  • Over a video

    In Impress a video plays in its own window and the laser would go behind it. The layer draws above it, so the laser never disappears.

  • Without a slideshow

    In a PDF, a browser or on the desktop, the laser, the pen and the black or white screen stay on, and so does the touchpad on your phone.

Over a video, several monitors, other scale settings and GNOME are not tried yet. The magnifier is Windows only.

Connect in one of three ways.

Every feature opens with QuickRemote PC. If you would rather install nothing on the computer, the phone connects to it like a keyboard. The watch uses the connection the phone makes.

Phone and QuickRemote PC

This way has the most features. The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network; no internet needed.

  1. Open QuickRemote PC on the computer and start the server.
  2. Scan the QR code on the phone. Computers on the same network also appear in a list, and you type the PIN once.
  3. Open your talk and tap Start on the phone. Next time you reconnect to your last computer with one tap.
  • Slide number, picture and notes
  • Laser, pen, highlighter
  • Laser layer and spotlight
  • Touchpad
  • Video and volume
  • Talk report
  • More than one phone

New: press and hold the app icon for a “Connect: Office-PC” shortcut, and add the tile to Quick Settings. Both connect to your last computer, and find it by name if its address has changed. Not yet tried on a phone.

Just the phone

You install nothing on the computer. The phone connects to it as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse.

  1. Tap Connect with Bluetooth in the app. If Bluetooth is off, the app offers to turn it on.
  2. Pick your phone in the computer’s Bluetooth settings and confirm the same 6-digit code on both. You do this once.
  3. Pick the presentation program: PowerPoint, Impress or WPS.

The computer does not answer a keyboard, so the slide number and notes do not reach the phone this way. If the computer does not accept the phone as a keyboard, the app shows what to do, step by step.

The watch, through your phone

The watch does not pair with the computer; it uses your phone’s Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connection.

  1. Connect to the computer on the phone and leave the remote screen open.
  2. Open QuickRemote on the watch. The remote appears by itself.
  3. Turn the bezel or tap the screen.

The slide number and the computer’s error messages reach the watch while the phone is on Wi-Fi. Over Bluetooth the watch shows “Bluetooth” in place of the slide number.

It runs quietly on the computer.

QuickRemote PC waits in the system tray; closing the window does not drop the phones that are connected.

  • Waits in the tray

    The icon’s color tells the state: grey off, green on, blue phone connected, amber held back on a public network.

  • On its own at login

    If you like, it starts in the tray without a window and starts the server too. On a public network the server never starts by itself.

  • Tells you about new versions

    It asks GitHub at launch and once a day; it only tells you, and downloads nothing. You can turn it off.

Everything stays on your network.

You open no account and go nowhere on the internet. The phone and the computer talk over an encrypted connection.

Encrypted connection
Every command sent over Wi-Fi is encrypted with TLS.
Local network only
Connections from outside the local network are refused before encryption even starts. Web browsers cannot connect either.
A new PIN at every start
Each time the server starts it makes a new 6-digit PIN, and it is replaced again when you remove a phone.
The QR code vouches for the computer
The phone expects the certificate in the QR code. Without a QR code it asks you to compare the security code.
Attempt limit
After 5 wrong PINs that device waits 60 seconds. After too many wrong attempts pairing pauses and the PIN is replaced.
The code stays off the screen
When the first phone connects, the QR code and PIN are hidden.
Connected phones
You see who is connected on the PC and can remove anyone.
The log stays with you
The error log stays on your device and goes nowhere by itself. PINs, pairing codes, notes and file paths are never written.

It works with the program you use.

The phone knows which window is in front and fades the tools that will not work, so you never tap something that does nothing.

Questions

If you cannot find your answer, ask on GitHub.

Does it cost anything?

No, QuickRemote is free, and you do not need an account.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. In Wi-Fi mode the phone and the computer only need to be on the same network. In Bluetooth mode you do not even need a network. The watch uses whichever the phone is connected with.

Does it work with an iPhone or Apple Watch?

Not for now. The remote app runs on Android, and the watch app on Wear OS.

Which watches does it work with?

Watches running Wear OS 3 and up. The watch works through QuickRemote on the Android phone it is paired with, and does not pair with the computer. The bezel on a Galaxy Watch and the crown on a Pixel Watch turn the slides. We tried the watch on a Galaxy Watch 6.

Do I have to install anything on the computer?

In Wi-Fi mode you need QuickRemote PC. In Bluetooth mode you install nothing; the computer sees the phone as a keyboard. The watch works through the phone in both modes.

Can I present from a PDF?

Yes. In Wi-Fi mode it opens full screen in Acrobat, SumatraPDF, Okular and browsers, and turns the pages. The phone shows a “Full screen” key, and the laser and pen work with QuickRemote’s layer. On Linux it can also turn a PDF open in LibreOffice into a presentation.

How does the slide’s picture reach the phone?

QuickRemote PC takes the slide from the program as a picture and sends it only to a phone that turned previews on; you can turn it off in Settings. The picture is the slide as saved: the current animation step, drawings and a playing video do not show. For full-screen shows and PDFs the screen itself is sent.

Can more than one phone connect at once?

Yes. The phone that picks up the laser or pen keeps it until its gesture ends. If two people press Next at the same time, the slide moves once.

Does the connection drop when the phone locks?

No. While the remote screen is open, the connection carries on in the background even if you lock the phone.

Does QuickRemote PC quit when I close its window?

By default, no: it goes to the tray and connected phones stay connected. To quit completely use Quit in the tray menu, or turn off “Minimize to tray on close” in Settings. On desktops without a tray, closing the window quits the app.

Does it collect data?

No. It sends no crash reports and no usage statistics. The error log stays on your device, and you attach it yourself if you want to share it. With the update check on, QuickRemote PC asks GitHub for a new version, and GitHub sees your IP address then; you can turn it off.

What do I do if something goes wrong?

On the phone: Settings → Help. On the PC: Settings → Troubleshooting. Share the error log, or tap “Report a problem” to open a pre-filled page on GitHub. You attach the log there yourself.

Which languages does it support?

Turkish and English. The apps follow the device’s language, and you can change it in Settings. On Android 13 and up you can also pick the app’s language in the system settings.

Download it free.

In Bluetooth mode the phone app is enough. For Wi-Fi mode you also install QuickRemote PC on the computer. The watch app works together with the one on the phone.

  • Android phone

    Download QuickRemote-…-android.apk from the releases page and open it. Android may ask you to allow installing from unknown sources. It is not on the Play Store yet.

    Releases page

  • Wear OS watch

    The …-wear-os.apk of the same release as the phone app. It is installed on the watch with adb; the phone and watch apps must be signed with the same key.

    adb -s <watch> install QuickRemote-…-wear-os.apk

  • Windows

    QuickRemote-PC-…-windows-x64-setup.exe: Windows 10 and 11, installed per user. The installer is unsigned, so if Windows shows a warning choose “Run anyway”. Not yet tried on Windows.

    Releases page

  • Linux

    A .deb for Debian, Ubuntu and Pardus, a .tar.gz for the rest. The package also adds the udev rule the virtual keyboard and mouse need.

    sudo apt install ./quickremote-pc_…_amd64.deb

    An AUR package, quickremote-pc-bin, is on its way for Arch.

The source code is on GitHub.
