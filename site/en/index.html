Connect one of three ways.

QuickRemote PC unlocks every feature. If you’d rather install nothing, your phone connects as a keyboard. The watch uses your phone’s connection.

Phone and QuickRemote PC

The most features. Your phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi network; no internet needed.

  1. Open QuickRemote PC on your computer.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone. Computers on the same network also show up in a list.
  3. Open your slides and tap Start on your phone.
  • Slide number and notes
  • Laser, pen, highlighter
  • Touchpad
  • Video and volume
  • Talk report
  • Several phones

Phone only

Nothing to install on the computer. Your phone connects to it as a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse.

  1. Tap Connect with Bluetooth in the app.
  2. Add your phone in the computer’s Bluetooth settings. You do this once.
  3. Pick your program: PowerPoint, Impress or WPS.

A computer doesn’t answer a keyboard, so the slide number and notes don’t reach your phone this way.

Watch, through your phone

The watch doesn’t pair with the computer; it uses your phone’s Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connection.

  1. Connect your phone to the computer and leave the remote screen open.
  2. Open QuickRemote on your watch. The remote appears on its own.
  3. Turn the bezel or tap the screen.

The slide number and the computer’s error messages reach the watch while your phone is on Wi-Fi.

Everything stays on your network.

No account, no internet. Your phone and computer talk over an encrypted connection.

Questions

Didn’t find your answer? Ask on GitHub.

Does it cost anything?

No, QuickRemote is free. You don’t need an account either.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. In Wi-Fi mode, your phone and computer just need the same network. Bluetooth mode needs no network at all. The watch uses whichever connection your phone has.

Does it work with iPhone or Apple Watch?

Not yet. The remote runs on Android and the watch app on Wear OS.

Which watches does it work with?

Watches running Wear OS 3 or later. The watch works through QuickRemote on its paired Android phone; it doesn’t pair with the computer. The bezel on a Galaxy Watch and the crown on a Pixel Watch change slides.

Do I need to install anything on the computer?

Wi-Fi mode needs QuickRemote PC. In Bluetooth mode you install nothing; the computer sees your phone as a keyboard. The watch works through your phone in both modes.

Can I present a PDF?

Yes. In Wi-Fi mode it opens full screen and turns pages in Acrobat, SumatraPDF, Okular and browsers. On Linux it can also turn a PDF open in LibreOffice into a presentation.

Can several phones connect at once?

Yes. The phone that takes the laser or pen first keeps it until its gesture ends. If two people press Next at the same time, the slide moves only once.

Does locking my phone drop the connection?

No. With the remote screen open, the connection keeps running in the background while your phone is locked.

Which languages does it support?

English and Turkish. The apps follow your device’s language; you can change it in settings.
